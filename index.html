HOW TO PLAY

MOVE & LOOKLeft thumb on the joystick, push it all the way to sprint. Swipe anywhere on the right side to look around.
AUTO SHOOTPoint roughly at an enemy: your aim locks on, tracks them and fires for you. Swipe hard to break the lock. The AUTO button turns it on/off.
JETPACKHold JUMP to fly. Fuel refills while you're on the ground, and you take no fall damage.
SHOCKWAVESelect it and tap fire to rocket up and forward. It also knocks back anyone next to you.
MEDKIT & SHIELDTap fire with a Medkit to heal to full. Your shield recharges out of combat, and every elimination fully heals you.
THE STORMStay inside the white circle on your map. Last player or team standing wins. In Reload Solo you get 2 respawns and earn one back every 3 eliminations (max 2). In Reload Duos and Squads you respawn while a teammate is still alive. Respawns end when Reload closes.
